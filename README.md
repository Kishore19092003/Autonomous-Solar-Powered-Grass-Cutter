# Autonomous Solar Powered Grass Cutter

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Autonomous Solar Powered Grass Cutter** is an eco-friendly automated grass-cutting system designed to reduce manual effort and provide efficient grass maintenance using solar energy.

The system combines solar power, a battery, motors, sensors, and a microcontroller to perform grass-cutting operations with reduced human intervention.

## 🎯 Objectives

* To develop an automated grass-cutting system.
* To utilize solar energy as a renewable power source.
* To reduce manual effort in grass cutting.
* To improve efficiency and convenience.
* To develop an environmentally friendly solution.

## ⚙️ Working Principle

The solar panel converts sunlight into electrical energy. The generated energy is used to charge the battery through the solar charge controller.

The battery supplies power to the control system and motors. The microcontroller controls the movement of the grass cutter and the cutting mechanism.

The sensors help the system detect obstacles and control its movement. The cutting motor rotates the cutting blade to cut the grass while the drive motors move the vehicle.
